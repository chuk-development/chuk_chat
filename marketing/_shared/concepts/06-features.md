# 06 — What Chuk Chat does (feature showcase)

- Job of this video: show the functions of the app. Nothing else. No privacy
  story, no price story, no joke. The viewer must leave with a list in their
  head: "research, charts, maps, images, files, documents, email, tools,
  assistants, models, Android, everywhere".
- Length: about 65 s. Music: `05-announce-yourself.wav` (upbeat electronic,
  120 BPM). Cut on the beat.
- Replaces video 01. Video 01's opening (a message is sent, nothing happens,
  the logo assembles) was rejected by the owner: "makes no sense".

## Rules for this video

- Show RESULTS, not the act of sending. The prompt is already a sent bubble
  when the beat starts (one short line, top of the card). The result builds
  in front of the viewer: the chart draws, the route draws, the image
  resolves, the PDF appears.
- No typing animation. No logo assembly. No "Introducing" title card.
- Each feature = one beat of 4–5 s with the same grammar, so the list reads:
  - left third: small mono counter ("03 / 13") + feature name ≥ 110 px +
    one plain line ≥ 40 px.
  - right two thirds: the result card, big (≥ 60% of frame height), lifted
    out of the app onto a warm backdrop (the 05 round-2 lift-out style: card
    on the site's worlds, soft shadow, no glow).
  - alternate the backdrop world per beat (paper, dawn, prism, pale green,
    café awning, night) so beats do not look the same.
- Every prompt and every result is unique in the series. Do NOT use the boss /
  burned-out prompt, Mrs Weber's invoice, or "announce yourself".

## Shape

0. Hook (0–3 s): frame 0 is already busy. A dense wall of 9 result cards
   (chart, map, weather, generated image, PDF, landing page, email card,
   model logos, connector logos) tiles in on the first beats, the camera
   pulls back. Headline in its own band: "One app. All of this."
1. Research the web — "Search the web and cite sources." Answer with
   numbered source chips (favicon + domain), "Searched 8 sources".
2. Live charts — "Charts from live data." A line chart draws (e.g. a stock or
   bitcoin 30-day line, see `assets/screenshots/screenshot_bitcoin_chart.webp`).
3. Maps and routes — "Places and routes on a map." Markers drop, then a route
   polyline draws (e.g. Kiel harbour → old town).
4. Weather — "Weather at a glance." Weather card with hourly strip (see
   `assets/screenshots/screenshot_weather_ui.webp`).
5. Create images — "Generate and edit images." The image resolves from noise.
   Use `marketing/_shared/images/generated-workshop.png` (AI-generated).
6. Read your files — "Ask your PDFs and photos." A PDF chip (lease.pdf) is
   attached, answer lists 3 key points with page refs.
7. Documents and pages — "Makes PDFs, pages and code." Artifact panel: a
   landing page with a public link (NOT the Spoke page from 01 — make a new
   one, e.g. a café menu page), then a code tab flip.
8. Email and calendar — "Drafts emails. Books the date." Email card with
   "Open in Mail App", then a calendar event card lands.
9. Your tools — "Works with Notion, Linear, GitHub and 50+ more." Connector
   logos slot in; a tool row shows "Ran notion search".
10. Your own assistants — "Workspaces with your files and rules." A workspace
    panel: name "Tax helper", instructions, 3 files; a chat answers inside it.
11. Pick the model — "Every open model. Switch per message." Model menu with
    the open-weight logos from `models.html`; the pick changes.
12. Android assistant — "Hold the home button. Ask." Phone with the
    assistant overlay (01 had a good version of this beat: reuse its look,
    new content).
13. Everywhere — "Mac, Windows, Linux, Android, Web." Device row; the same
    chat on laptop and phone; "Synced and end-to-end encrypted."
14. End (≈60–65 s): "€20 / month · €16 AI credits included" line, then logo +
    "Chuk Chat" + "Private and Secure. Always." + "chuk.chat". Static, clean,
    on the beat. No logo animation gimmick.

## Allowed feature claims (from README.md and docs/FEATURES.md)

Web search with sources, inline charts (bar, line, pie, scatter, radar),
inline maps with markers and routes, weather cards with hourly forecast, image
generation and editing, file attachments (images, PDFs, documents), artifacts
(landing pages with public links, PDF documents, code), email drafts with
"Open in Mail App", calendar events and reminders, MCP connectors (50+),
workspaces (custom instructions + files as context), open-weight model picker
per message, Android assistant (hold home), desktop/Android/web, end-to-end
encrypted sync. Plus the claims in SERIES.md.
