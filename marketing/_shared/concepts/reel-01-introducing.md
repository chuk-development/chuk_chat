# Reel 01 — Introducing Chuk Chat (Muse-style launch reel)

- Format: 1080x1920 (9:16), 30 fps, about 55–60 s. Instagram Reels / Shorts.
- Reference: Meta "Introducing Muse" reel,
  `_scratch/reel_ref/DdCYlleiW5S.mp4` (contact sheets `sheet.jpg`,
  `sheet2.jpg`, `sheet3.jpg` next to it). Copy its FORMAT and RHYTHM, never
  its assets, mascot, colours or music.
- Music: `marketing/_shared/music/05-announce-yourself.wav` (upbeat, 120 BPM).
  Also render a version with NO music (the owner adds a trending IG sound).
- No voice-over.

## What makes the reference work (keep all of it)

1. Frame 0: the app icon alone, then a burst of 3D objects flies out of it.
2. Kinetic headline, word by word, with small 3D icons popping in BETWEEN the
   words ("Muse [mascot] is your [calendar] personal AI [book] agent").
3. Chat scenes with NO phone frame: big floating bubbles on a clean
   background, the assistant avatar with a live status line ("is working",
   "Adding to cart"), result cards that slide in, and a big "Done ✓".
4. Between scenes, a repeated list pattern: "AI that [icon] goes shopping /
   books meetings / drafts replies … for you" — one verb phrase per beat.
5. "Connect your apps" → app logos converge → "All in one place" with check
   marks.
6. Clean end: icon + name + where to get it.

## Our version (brand: warm paper #FDFBF7, ink #26251F, coral #D97757;
bottom of frame a soft warm dawn gradient instead of Muse's blue)

1. 0–2 s: the Chuk Chat logo tile. A burst of 3D objects (laptop, calendar,
   envelope, map, chart, padlock, receipt, phone, paint palette) flies out.
   Use Microsoft Fluent 3D emoji (MIT licence,
   https://github.com/microsoft/fluentui-emoji, `assets/<Name>/3D/*.png`).
2. 2–5 s: "Introducing" / "Chuk Chat" (coral).
3. 5–9 s: "Chuk Chat is your [padlock] private [speech] AI [laptop] chat".
4. 9–11 s: "One private chat. Many worlds." (the website headline).
5. 11–22 s: scene "week": user "This week is a mess." → Chuk Chat "Want me
   to sort it?" → user "Check Linear, Todoist and Notion. Make me a plan." →
   status "Checking Linear… Todoist… Notion" with their logos → plan card
   (Mon: fix the login bug · Tue: send the report · Thu: review the launch
   plan) → "Done ✓".
6. 22–27 s: "AI that [icon] plans your week / writes the invoice / drafts
   your emails / researches the web — for you".
7. 27–38 s: scene "invoice": "Write the invoice for Mrs Weber: kitchen shelf,
   6 hours at €58, material €140. As PDF." → status "Compiling document" →
   PDF card (Total €488.00) → user "Now email it to her." → email card with
   "Open in Mail App" → "Done ✓".
8. 38–42 s: "AI that [icon] charts your data / maps your route / makes images /
   reads your PDFs — for you".
9. 42–50 s: scene "Android": text "Hold the home button." → assistant overlay
   over a phone home screen: "Find a pharmacy that is open now." → 3 results
   → "Navigation started" → "Done ✓". (This is the one place a phone frame
   is allowed, because the overlay is the feature.)
10. 50–53 s: "Connect your apps" → Notion, Linear, GitHub, Todoist, Dropbox,
    Figma logos converge → "All in one place ✓".
11. 53–56 s: "AI that [padlock] keeps your chats encrypted / never trains on
    them".
12. 56–60 s: logo tile + "Chuk Chat" + "Private and Secure. Always." +
    "chuk.chat" + "Mac · Windows · Linux · Android · Web".

1080x1920 canvas. Keep 120 px safe margins top and bottom (IG UI overlays).
