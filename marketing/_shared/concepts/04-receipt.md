# 04 — The receipt

- Length: about 40 s. Music: `04-receipt.wav` (marimba, pizzicato, ticking,
  deadpan stops, 112 BPM).
- Message: "No hype. Here is exactly what you get."
- Angle: anti-hype. Funny and dry.

## Shape

1. Parody hook (0–4 s): a fake generic AI hype frame: purple space glow,
   lens flare, huge text "THE MOST POWERFUL AI EVER". (This is the only glow
   allowed in the series, and it is a joke. No real brand.)
2. Record stop (4–5 s): hard cut to warm paper and silence for a beat. Mono
   text: "Ours is shorter."
3. Receipt (5–33 s): a narrow thermal receipt prints from the top, line by
   line, on the beat, with a small printer tick. Monospace, dotted leaders:
   CHUK CHAT — THE SHORT VERSION
   Your chats ........ encrypted on your device
   Training on your data ........ 0
   Tracking ........ 0
   Models ........ open-weight only
   Model switch ........ every message
   Connectors ........ 50+
   Open source ........ 100%
   Price ........ €20 / month
   incl. AI credits ........ €16
   From ........ Germany
   Then a TOTAL line: "Private and Secure. Always."
   The camera follows the paper down. Punch in on the funny lines ("0").
4. Tear (33–36 s): the receipt tears off. The barcode at the bottom reads
   "chuk.chat".
5. End card (36–40 s).
