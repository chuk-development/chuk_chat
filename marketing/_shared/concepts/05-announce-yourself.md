# 05 — "Chuk, announce yourself"

- Length: about 60 s. Music: `05-announce-yourself.wav` (upbeat electronic,
  118 BPM, two risers, drop near the end).
- Message: "One chat that does it all, privately."
- Angle: the whole trailer happens inside a Chuk Chat conversation. This is
  the series format: later videos are new prompts ("Chuk, compare yourself",
  "Chuk, show Agents"). Build it so that the frame is reusable.

## Shape

1. Hook (0–4 s): the dark Chuk Chat desktop window on a painted sky. The
   user types: "Announce yourself on YouTube. Keep it short." Send on a beat.
2. Working (4–7 s): "Worked for 3s", tool rows appear ("Ran create artifact"…).
3. The answer builds the trailer (7–50 s). The reply streams in, and each
   part is a real app card, cut fast on the beat, camera punches in on each:
   - Model picker card cycles through the open-weight model logos
     (from `models.html`): "Pick a model for each message."
   - Connector row: logos slide in: "Drives Notion, Linear, GitHub and 50+ more."
   - Artifact panel: the landing page "Spoke — Bike repair at your door"
     from the founders world renders: "Builds pages, PDFs, code."
   - Invoice PDF from the business world: "Does the office work."
   - Encryption card: text turns to ciphertext: "Encrypted on your device."
   - Price line: "€20 / month. €16 AI credits included."
   At the drop the reply ends with one big line: "Private and Secure. Always."
4. Pull-out (50–55 s): the window scales down and the sky fills the frame.
5. End card (55–60 s).
