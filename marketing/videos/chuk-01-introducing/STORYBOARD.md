  (moon night, dawn, café awning, network, prism, street dusk). The dark app window is the constant
  hero; it keeps one size and position across the four desktop worlds, so the world changes around it.

  headline beat on the painted sky — mono eyebrow 30 px goldenrod + Arimo headline 104 px, two
  balanced lines — then the headline leaves and the app window rises into a close framing.
  Headline and UI never share the screen (frame 08 keeps the headline in its own left column).
- Legibility: the app mock is scaled ×1.75, so 16 px chat, bubble, answer and tool-row text renders
  at 28 px; timeline labels were raised to 16 px logical. The full window fills the frame
  (1750×980), so there is no small-window-in-empty-space shot. Punch-ins go to ×1.07–×1.13.
- Held beats: the title (2.4 s hold), the proof chips (last 2.4 s), the end card (3.3 s).
- No glow, no coloured shadow. Only dark warm drop shadows under windows.
