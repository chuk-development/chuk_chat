Chuk Chat is an AI chat client built for people who want to know what runs their AI and where their words end up.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Chats that sync to the server are encrypted on your device with AES-256-GCM before they leave it. We cannot read them, even if the server is compromised. Your encryption key never leaves the device. Release builds write no logs.

OPEN-WEIGHT MODELS ONLY
DeepSeek, Llama, Mistral, Qwen and more. No closed black boxes, no silent data collection, and you pick the model per conversation.

BUILT FOR REAL WORK
- Streaming answers you can read as they are written
- Tool calling: web search, news, weather, places, calculator, notes, QR codes
- Image generation and editing right inside the chat
- Interactive charts and maps rendered inline from the model's answer
- File attachments: images, PDFs and documents
- Offline access to your history, with automatic recovery when the network returns

MAKE IT YOURS
Theme packs, accent colors, contrast, light and dark mode, and three bundled reading fonts. Material You picks up your system colors if you want it to.

EVERYWHERE
The same app on Android, iOS, Linux, macOS, Windows and the web. Your chats follow you.

A NOTE ON YOUR PASSWORD
If you lose it, your synced chats are gone. There is no recovery path, and that is the point: nobody else can open them either. Pick a strong one and keep it safe.
